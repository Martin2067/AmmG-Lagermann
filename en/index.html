Lagermann WMS

You run your business. Lagermann runs your warehouse.

A modern warehouse management system for small operations, warehouses, wholesalers, manufacturers, online retailers and logistics centres.

  • Visibility of stock and storage locations
  • Goods receipt, goods issue and stock transfers
  • Stocktakes and stock movement history
  • QR codes and barcodes
  • Multiple warehouses and user roles
  • Reports and oversight of warehouse processes
